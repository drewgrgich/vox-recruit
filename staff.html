HOW TO REDEEM

1. Type the serial from the physical card, not from their phone screen.

2. Type the code from their phone. Dashes and capitals don't matter.

3. VALID? Hand over the swag, then punch, sign or keep the card. That's what stops a second redemption. This phone also remembers what it marked, but the other phones don't.

STUCK OR GLITCHED RECRUIT

If someone legitimately finished but their phone died or lost progress, you can look up their card's code here (PIN required). In the recruit app itself, holding the serial chip in the top-right for 2 seconds skips the current test with the same PIN.
